Meet Dr. Patel!

Dr. Vidisha Patel founded Little Buds Pediatric Dentistry to give children in Midlothian a place where they can feel comfortable and confident at the dentist. With compassion and patience at the heart of every visit, she's dedicated to helping your child build healthy habits and a positive relationship with dental care that lasts a lifetime.
