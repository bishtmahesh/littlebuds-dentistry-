Hi, I’m Dr. Patel!

I founded Little Buds Pediatric Dentistry to give children in Midlothian and throughout Chesterfield County a place where they can feel comfortable and confident at the dentist.

Compassion and patience are at the heart of every visit. My goal is to help your child build healthy habits and a positive relationship with dental care that lasts a lifetime, and to make sure you always understand what we’re doing and why.

Whether it’s your baby’s very first checkup or your teen’s last visit before college, I’d love to be part of your family’s dental journey.

Questions about Dr. Patel

What is a pediatric dentist?

A pediatric dentist completes additional years of specialty training after dental school, focused entirely on the dental health of infants, children and teens, including children with special health care needs.

What does board-certified mean?

A board-certified pediatric dentist has passed the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry’s rigorous exams and keeps up with ongoing education to maintain that certification.

How does Dr. Patel help nervous children?

She explains each step in kid-friendly words, shows tools before using them, and goes at your child’s pace, celebrating every brave moment along the way.

Does Dr. Patel see children with special needs?

Yes. Dr. Patel and our team welcome children with unique needs and are happy to plan extra time and any adjustments that help your child feel comfortable.

Until what age does Dr. Patel see patients?

Dr. Patel sees children from their very first tooth through their teenage years, and can help with a smooth move to a general dentist when the time comes.
