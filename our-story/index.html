Our Story

Little Buds grew from a simple idea: a trip to the dentist should feel safe, kind and even a little bit fun.

Book an appointmentCall (804) 601-0469

A dental home for growing smiles

Dr. Vidisha Patel opened Little Buds so children in Midlothian and across Chesterfield County would have a place where they feel comfortable and confident at the dentist.

Too many kids carry a fear of the dentist into adulthood. We want the opposite: children who walk in smiling, parents who feel heard, and healthy habits that last a lifetime. Every part of the practice, from how we talk to kids to how our rooms look and feel, is built around that goal.

We see children from their very first tooth through their teenage years, including kids with unique needs who need a little extra time and care.

What we believe

Four promises we make to every family who walks through our door.

You come first

Saturday appointments, easy communication and a team that actually listens. Your family’s schedule and your child’s comfort always come first.

Gentle, modern care

Safe, up-to-date tools and techniques that keep things gentle. We choose minimally invasive options whenever we can.

Real expertise, real heart

A board-certified pediatric dentist and a warm, patient team who meet every child right where they are.

Made for kids

Soft colors, playful touches, a cozy sensory nook and ceiling TVs, all designed to help kids relax.

What a visit feels like

  • We explain every step in kid-friendly words before we do it.
  • Parents are welcome to stay close, especially for little ones.
  • We go at your child’s pace and celebrate every brave moment.
  • You leave with simple, practical tips for brushing and eating at home.

Getting to know Little Buds

Who is Little Buds for?

Little Buds is a dental home just for kids. We see children from their very first tooth through their teenage years, including children with unique needs who need a little extra time and care.

Where are you located?

We’re at 5065 Craig Rath Blvd, Midlothian, VA 23112, serving families in Midlothian and throughout Chesterfield County.

Do you offer Saturday appointments?

Yes. Saturday visits are available by appointment only. Give us a call and we’ll find a time that works for your family.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes, we accept most major PPO dental plans. See our Insurance & Financial Info page, or call us and we’ll help check your coverage.

How do I book my child’s first visit?

Use the appointment form on this page or call us at (804) 601-0469. We’ll confirm a time and let you know what to bring.
