Questions parents ask us

When should my child first see a dentist?

We recommend a first visit by your child’s first birthday, or within six months of their first tooth coming in, whichever comes first.

How often should my child have a checkup?

Most children do best with a checkup and cleaning every six months. We’ll let you know if your child would benefit from a different schedule.

What if my child is nervous about the dentist?

That’s completely normal. Our team is trained to help anxious kids feel safe, and we always go at a pace that works for your child.

Until what age do you see patients?

We see patients through their teenage years and can help with a smooth move to a general dentist when the time comes.
