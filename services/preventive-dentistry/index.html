Is your child due for preventive care?

Preventive care is the routine care that keeps teeth strong and stops problems before they start. It’s a good time to book a visit if:

  • It’s been more than six months since your child’s last checkup
  • You notice white, brown or chalky spots on their teeth
  • Their gums look red or bleed when they brush
  • They snack often or sip sugary drinks through the day

Book a checkup using the form below or call us at (804) 601-0469. Most children do best with a visit every six months.

Good to know: early cavities often can’t be seen or felt, so even teeth that look healthy benefit from regular checkups. Routine visits like these can help prevent up to 80% of childhood cavities.

Preventive Dentistry FAQs

Straight answers to the questions parents ask us most.

How often should my child have a checkup?

Every six months works well for most children. If your child is more prone to cavities, we may suggest visiting a little more often.

Is fluoride safe for kids?

Yes. Used in the right amounts, fluoride is a safe and proven way to strengthen teeth and prevent cavities. We’ll recommend the right amount for your child’s age.

When should my child start flossing?

Start flossing once two teeth touch each other. Floss picks can make it easier, and we’re happy to show you how at your child’s visit.

What snacks are best for teeth?

Cheese, yogurt, fresh fruit and crunchy vegetables are great choices. Choose water between meals and keep sticky or sugary snacks for mealtimes.

How much toothpaste should my child use?

A smear the size of a grain of rice for children under 3, and a pea-sized amount from age 3. Help with brushing until your child can do a thorough job on their own, usually around age 7 or 8.
