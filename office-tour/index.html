Visiting our office

Where is the office?

You’ll find us at 5065 Craig Rath Blvd, Midlothian, VA 23112. Tap “Get directions” above to open the route in Google Maps.

What are your office hours?

Monday and Thursday 8:30am–5pm, Friday 8:30am–1:30pm, and Tuesday and Saturday by appointment only. We’re closed on Wednesdays.

What should we bring to our first visit?

Please bring your insurance card, a photo ID, and any dental records or x-rays from a previous dentist. Completing new patient forms ahead of time also helps.

Do you see children with special needs?

Yes. Let us know about any sensory, medical or behavioral needs when you book, and we’ll plan extra time and the right setting for your child.

How can I help my child feel ready for their first visit?

Look at these photos together, keep the conversation light and positive, and let them know the dentist will count and clean their teeth. We take care of the rest.
