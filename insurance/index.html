Payments & policies

A few simple policies that help us keep visits running smoothly for every family.

Payment at your visit

Payment is due at the time of service unless other arrangements have been made. We’ll always review expected costs with you before any treatment.

Ways to pay

We accept cash, debit cards and major credit cards. Flexible financing may be available if you’d like to spread out the cost of treatment.

Cancellation policy

Life happens! Please give us at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or reschedule, so we can offer the time to another family.

Clear cost estimates

Before treatment begins, we’ll walk you through your options and an estimate of what insurance covers and what you’ll pay.

Insurance questions

Do you file insurance claims for us?

Yes. We’ll send claims to your insurance company for you and help you understand what your plan covers.

Do I need to bring anything to the first visit?

Please bring your insurance card, a photo ID, and any recent dental records or x-rays from a previous dentist.

What if my child has two insurance plans?

Let us know about both plans when you book. We’ll coordinate benefits so you get the most out of your coverage.

What if our insurance changes?

Just let us know before your next visit. We’ll update your records and help confirm your new coverage so there are no surprises.
